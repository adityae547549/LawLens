Before You Rely On It

Know the limitations

What you should verify on your own.

Not Legal Advice

AI-generated explanations are research aids, not counsel. Consult a qualified professional for decisions about your specific situation.

Curated Corpus

The database covers a curated set of Acts and judgments. Missing text isn't proof a law doesn't exist — check India Code directly.

Verify Primary Sources

Statutes and amendments change. Cross-check dates, amendments, and citations against official gazettes and court websites.

Translations May Vary

Multilingual answers are AI-translated. The authoritative text of every Indian law is the official English/Hindi version.
