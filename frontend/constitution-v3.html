Constitution of India

Article 21

Protection of Life and Personal Liberty

Part III - Fundamental Rights Chapter - Right to Life

No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.

AI Explanation
Summary
Article 21 guarantees the right to life and personal liberty to all persons. It states that no person can be deprived of these rights except through a procedure established by law.
Key Points
  • • Applies to all persons, not just citizens
  • • "Procedure established by law" requires fair procedure
  • • Expanded by judicial interpretation to include right to privacy
