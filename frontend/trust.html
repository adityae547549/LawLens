Trust & Security

Trust & Security

Your privacy, data security, and trust in AI accuracy are our top priorities.

Data Privacy & Security

Is my data encrypted?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your server. Communication uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Go to Settings → Clear All History to delete all your conversations, searches, and bookmarks. You can also delete individual items.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your data stays on your server. We don't share it with third parties. Your queries are processed by Groq AI and are not stored by them.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Go to Settings → Export My Data to download all your conversations, bookmarks, and search history as JSON.

AI Transparency

Does AI explain its sources?

Yes. Every answer includes [Source N] citations that link directly to the original legal documents. You can click any source to view the original text.

Can I verify every citation?

Yes. Every source citation is clickable and links to the original document chunk. You can always verify what the AI cited.

Can AI admit when it doesn't know?

Yes. The AI is programmed to say "I don't have enough information" when it cannot find verified information from the sources. It will never guess or hallucinate.

Does the app warn that it's not legal advice?

Yes. Every AI response includes a disclaimer: "This is AI-generated information from legal documents. It is not legal advice."

How accurate is the AI?

Every response includes a confidence score (0-100%) based on source quality. Official sources (Constitution, SC) get higher trust. You always know how confident the AI is.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LawLens a substitute for a lawyer?
No. LawLens is a research tool that helps you understand legal documents and concepts. It is not legal advice and should not replace consultation with a qualified legal professional.
Can I use LawLens answers in court?
AI-generated answers should not be directly submitted in court. They are research aids. Always verify citations with official sources and consult your lawyer.
How does the AI avoid hallucinations?
The AI only answers from retrieved legal documents. If information is not found, it explicitly says so. Every fact must have a citation from the source documents.
What if I find an incorrect answer?
You can report incorrect answers by clicking the "Report" button on any AI response. This helps us improve the system. Always verify with official sources.
Is my data sent to AI companies?
Your queries are sent to Groq AI for processing. Groq does not store your queries after processing. Your stored data (conversations, bookmarks) stays on your server.
